Trade Checklists

Deduction guides built for your specific industry

The IRS does not advertise legitimate deductions. We compiled comprehensive checklists for the professions we serve most across Maryland, Virginia, and Washington DC.

Real Estate

Realtors & Brokers

Desk fees, MLS dues, staging costs, vehicle mileage, open house expenses, continuing education, and client gifts under Section 274.

Trades & Construction

Contractors & Builders

Heavy equipment depreciation, tool write-offs, subcontractor 1099 compliance, job site fuel, uniforms, and builder’s risk insurance.

Transportation

Per diem meal deductions (80% rate), sleeper berth supplies, electronic logging devices (ELD), fuel tax (IFTA), and highway use tax (Form 2290).

Medical supplies, caregiver training, mileage reimbursement under an accountable plan, PPE, and licensing fees.
